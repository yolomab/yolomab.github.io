What's happening

Simplified model: 10 H⁺ per NADH, 6 per FADH₂, 4 H⁺ per ATP (3 through the rotor + 1 to import phosphate), so ≈2.5 and ≈1.5 ATP each. CoQ picks up 2 H⁺ from the matrix and releases them at Complex III.
