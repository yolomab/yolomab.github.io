    Method

    Prices are Polymarket's displayed prices (the order-book midpoint, or the last trade when the spread is wide), polled every 15 seconds from the public Gamma API. History comes from the CLOB prices-history endpoint.

    Seat brackets are separate yes/no markets, so their prices are normalised to sum to 100%. Open-ended brackets get an assumed width: “<20” for Likud is read as 15–19, “35+” as 35–39. A “<4” bracket means missing the 3.25% threshold and winning 0 seats.

    Simulation: each run draws a seat count for every party from its bracket distribution, adds the Reservists–New Economic Party (no seat market) using its “wins a seat” price and its vote-share brackets, then rescales all parties to 120 seats by largest remainder. Draws are independent before rescaling, so the model ignores swings that move several parties together. Treat it as a reading of the markets, not a forecast.

    Vote-share route: expected vote share converted to seats with 120 × share ÷ (100 − expected % of vote below threshold).
